    How to play

    • Goal: land as many planes as you can. The game ends when two planes get too close.
    • Heading is the direction a plane flies, in degrees on a compass: 0 is north, 90 is east, 180 is south, 270 is west.
    • Altitude is height above the ground, in feet (ft).
    • Speed is in knots (kt). One knot is about 1.85 km/h.
    • Distance is in nautical miles (nm). One nautical mile is about 1.85 km. The rings on the radar are 10 nm apart.
    • Too close means less than 3 nm apart sideways and less than 1000 ft apart in height. A plane turns red and shows "!" when it is getting close to another one.
    • Landing: the runway points east (heading 090). A plane can land if it is on the dashed line west of the airport (within 12 nm), heading about 090, at 2000 ft or lower and 180 kt or slower. Then send LAND.
    • Landing a plane scores 100. A plane that flies off the screen costs 50.

    Commands

    • BA12 H 270 turn to heading 270 (0 to 360).
    • BA12 A 5000 climb or descend to 5000 ft (1000 to 12000).
    • BA12 S 180 change speed to 180 kt (120 to 300).
    • BA12 LAND try to land.

    Or tap a plane, then use the buttons.

    This is a simplified game, not real training material.
